GovOS – Governance & contracts

GovOS is the governance layer: it turns decisions into statements of work, payment flows, and policy artefacts.

  • SoW generation and AISDR-AUTH flows.
  • Stripe-backed payment & confirmation workers.
  • Designed to share proofs with Codex & OceanOS.

Together with Codex and OceanOS, GovOS forms a governance OS spanning forecasts, the physical world, and contractual execution.
